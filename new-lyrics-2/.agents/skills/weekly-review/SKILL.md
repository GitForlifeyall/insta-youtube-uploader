# Weekly review

Read `creator-workflow` and list the active project-brain pages. Ask the creator to open the separate Creator Vault as an allowed workspace folder if it is not already available.

Summarize only:

- Finished and blocked song projects.
- Render problems that repeated.
- Confirmed creator preferences.
- Decisions that should become durable brain pages.
- The next three highest-value actions.

Do not scan raw videos, audio, fonts, or full lyrics. Keep the review to one page and retain creator judgment where visual taste or music selection is involved.
